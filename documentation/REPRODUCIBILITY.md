The original workflow comprises infrastructure reconstruction and simulations, demand grids, road topology and routing, fixed and mobile coverage, optimization, robustness and sensitivity evaluation, principal computational reproduction and publication consolidation.

Phase 12B reran 60 principal allocations from saved upstream inputs. The 37,920 compared exported numerical values agreed at recorded precision. This was not a raw-data-to-results rerun or an independent external replication. The extended 145-configuration evaluation was not rerun in Phase 12B. Twelve restricted exact benchmarks were verified; ten of twelve national LP relaxations converged. National allocations remain heuristic. Historical Phase 8B1 figure attribution remains unresolved.
