# Ghana robust mobile imaging

Research materials for robust equity-aware spatial optimization of hypothetical mobile diagnostic imaging services under infrastructure uncertainty across Ghana's 16 regions.

## Scope
The study compares conventional, equity-aware, robust, and combined robust equity-aware allocation of X-ray, ultrasound and co-located imaging services. It uses probabilistic fixed-service availability and road-network accessibility. The extended evaluation covers 145 uncertainty configurations. These are simulated planning outcomes, not verified operational sites or clinical outcomes.

## Reproducibility
The principal 60 allocations were reproduced from saved upstream inputs. Full raw-data-to-results reproduction and portable execution have not been established. Third-party source reuse conditions and code provenance must be checked before further release. No blanket licence is asserted.

## Archive policy
The manuscript, all figures, private audit records, and raw third-party datasets are excluded. This repository is a work in progress, not a certified standalone reproduction package.
